Given Structure

Condition: Beam with two equal spans L, loads P1 and P2, and end moment P3.

Step 3: Identify Vectors

{f} = [b]{P}

Step 4: Member Flexibility

For the beam element, enter the outside multiplier of [F].

[F] = k 2-100-120-1002-10-1-12

Step 5: Transformation Matrix

[b] is obtained from the force transformation relation.

[b] = L2L1-L0-10L1001

Enter selected values of [b].

Step 6: Total Structural Flexibility

[F]TS = [b]T[F]c[b]

[F]TS = L/(6EI) 2L²5L²3L5L²16L²12L3L12L12

Enter selected terms of [F]TS.

Step 7: External Displacement Vector

{u} = [F]TS{P}

Use the first row of the matrix to calculate u1.

Enter u1.

u1 = (2L³P1 + 5L³P2 + 3L²P3)/(6EI)
